# Customer_Churn_Analysis
Customer Churn Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer churn patterns, identifying high-risk customer segments, and generating actionable business insights. 

Customer churn directly affects a company's revenue, customer lifetime value, and long-term growth.

The key business challenge is:

Why are customers leaving, which customer segments are at higher risk of churn, and what factors can businesses monitor to improve customer retention?
