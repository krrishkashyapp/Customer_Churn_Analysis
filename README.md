# Customer_Churn_Analysis
Customer Churn Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer churn patterns, identifying high-risk customer segments, and generating actionable business insights. 

🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn directly affects a company's revenue, customer lifetime value, and long-term growth.
The key business challenge is:

Why are customers leaving, which customer segments are at higher risk of churn, and what factors can businesses monitor to improve customer retention?

This project analyzes customer demographics, subscription details, contracts, services, payment methods, tenure and monthly charges to identify potential churn patterns.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

🧹 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation

Python was used to prepare the dataset before analysis.

Key activities included:

1. Handling missing and inconsistent values.
2. Removing unnecessary spaces from categorical values.
3. Standardizing text/categorical fields.
4. Checking data types.
5. Cleaning numerical columns.
6. Preparing categorical variables for analysis.
7. Validating the final dataset before visualization.

🗄️ 2. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to answer business-oriented questions from the cleaned customer dataset.

💡 Business Questions the Dashboard Can Answer:-

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?

Helps management understand the scale of customer attrition and monitor whether retention strategies are improving performance.

2. Which contract type has the highest number of churned customers?

This can help the business identify whether customers with particular contract structures are more likely to leave.

Business Impact:
The company can evaluate contract flexibility, renewal strategies and incentives for high-risk contract segments.

3. Which subscription type contributes the most to churn?

Understanding churn by subscription type helps identify whether a particular pricing or service package requires attention.

Business Impact:
Businesses can optimize plans, pricing and promotional offers.

4. Which states have the highest churn or revenue contribution?

The dashboard enables geographical comparison of customer activity and revenue.

Business Impact:
Management can prioritize regional retention campaigns and investigate location-specific customer issues.

5. Does customer tenure appear to influence churn?

Comparing tenure and churn can help identify whether newer customers are more vulnerable to leaving.

Business Impact:
If early-tenure customers show higher churn, the company could introduce stronger onboarding, engagement and early-retention programs.

6. Are customers with higher monthly charges more likely to churn?

The monthly charges vs churn analysis helps investigate the relationship between customer spending and churn behavior.

Business Impact:
The company can evaluate pricing, discounts and perceived value among higher-paying customers.

7. Which internet service category has higher churn?

The dashboard breaks down churn according to internet service.

Business Impact:
This can help identify service categories where customer satisfaction, pricing or service quality may require improvement.

8. Which payment methods are associated with more customers?

Payment-method analysis provides insight into how customers prefer to make payments.

Business Impact:
Businesses can optimize payment experiences and investigate whether certain payment processes create friction.

9. How does churn differ between senior and non-senior customers?

The dashboard allows customer behavior to be compared across senior-citizen segments.

Business Impact:
Businesses can design more targeted communication and customer support strategies for different demographic groups.

10. Which customer segments should be prioritized for retention?

By combining filters such as:

Age + State + Subscription Type + Contract + Internet Service + Payment Method

the business can drill down into specific customer segments.

Business Impact:
Instead of applying the same retention strategy to everyone, businesses can develop targeted retention campaigns.
